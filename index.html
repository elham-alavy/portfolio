01

About me

Think beyond buildings.
Engineer better solutions.

My work brings together environmental science, engineering, and data analysis. At the University of Arizona, I explore how buildings and environmental systems shape climate resilience and human well-being.

My current contributions span digital twin platforms, building heat stress analytics, solar suitability, electrification, and interdisciplinary course development. Across these projects, I connect research questions with the data pipelines and analytical tools needed to investigate them.
